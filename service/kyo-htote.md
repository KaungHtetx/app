# Kyo Htote

### 💼 Kyo Htote Loan Application – MoMoney App

#### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Access Service**
   * Log in to your **MoMoney** app.
   * From the home screen, tap the **Kyo Htote** service.
2. **Verify Information**
   * The system displays:
     * Company information
     * Staff name
     * NRC number
   * Confirm all details are correct.
3. **Enter Loan Amount**
   * Input your desired loan amount.
   * Ensure it is within your available limit.
   * Review applicable fees and interest.
4. **Submit Application**
   * Tap the **Apply** button.
   * Carefully read all **Terms & Conditions**.
5. **Signature Process**
   * Choose one of the following:
     * Draw your signature on screen, **OR**
     * Upload a signature image.
6. **Final Confirmation**
   * Review all transaction details:
     * Loan amount
     * Fees
     * Repayment terms
   * Press the **Confirm** button.
7. **Authentication**
   * Enter your **6-digit security PIN** to authorize the loan disbursement.
8. **Completion**
   * A **successful transaction** page will be displayed.
